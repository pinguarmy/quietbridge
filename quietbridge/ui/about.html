About QuietBridge

What if speaking isn't an option? QuietBridge offers another way to put a message into words. Type a message, or try silent mouth movements and supported gestures. Review the words before choosing to show or speak them.

British Sign Language in the UK

British Sign Language (BSL) is a visual language with its own grammar. People use handshapes, facial expressions and body movement to communicate.[1]

RNID reports the British Deaf Association's estimate of about 151,000 people who use BSL in the UK. Estimates vary by definition; this is not an exact count of daily use.[2]

The Royal College of Speech and Language Therapists estimates that up to 14 million people in the UK will experience communication difficulties at some point in their lives. This includes difficulties such as understanding, reading or writing; it is not the number of people currently unable to speak.[3]

QuietBridge does not translate full sign-language conversations. An optional sign model handles isolated sign concepts, not fingerspelling or sentences.
